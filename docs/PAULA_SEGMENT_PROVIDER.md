# Typed serialized HOST provider

The adapter owns four once-only big-endian model records while preserving each original ticket, window and frame. Its explicit label table uses model numbers; these numbers are not native addresses and cache_base32 is never interpreted as a HOST pointer.

Injected resource callbacks provide modeled enter/restore, clock, packet, ticket, reader quiet and source quiet operations. Whole-entry serialization forbids callback reentry, including during restore. Packet quiet, reader quiet and source quiet are independent obligations. Unknown callback or restoration results latch failure and retain storage; observed returned entry tokens do not grant restoration authority. Original packet and genuine owner storage remain live through the required quiet proofs and positive outer restoration.

Known resource-context, descriptor, master, cache and guard extents are excluded from all caller outputs, including before a first record is published and after record retirement. This covers status, row, enter-token and clock outputs without treating the model label table as host addresses. The HOST75 fixture includes 45 genuine alias designs and verifies unchanged refused outputs, no provider effects and preserved ownership.

The exact V4 private HOST75 compile and fixture passed with C99, assertions, warnings as errors, AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer. The formatting-only V4 successor preserves the earlier compiler-only V3 failure as separate history. The renamed packet32 donor main is not invoked, so this fixture does not claim its separate46-case suite or the future60 suite.

The canonical provider and its exact75-case fixture also passed once under the same strict sanitizer flags. The on-disk strict driver keeps ROOT_ADMITTED and FUTURE_HOST_QUALIFIED false and the future acceptance pin empty; the trial used separately reviewed ephemeral admission and the exact previously qualified future prerequisite. No frontend, PLAY default, general song scheduler, retiming or jitter policy is added.

Native builds remain inhibited. The HOST contract refuses delivery reentry during restore, whereas the older rolling IRQ seam permits deferred delivery on restoration. A real serialization and mutation seam still needs an explicit implementation and qualification; no casts bridge these contracts. Amiberry, A1200, hardware timing, audio and listening acceptance are not established by this HOST result.
