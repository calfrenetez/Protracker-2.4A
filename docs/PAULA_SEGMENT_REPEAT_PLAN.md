Ranges are half-open master frames. One mono signed8 cache frame is one playback byte; programmed starts and lengths must be even, with lengths 2..131070 bytes or 1..65535 words. Master capacity is an int32-element count and protects its complete byte extent. Even cache padding is protected but is never selected as an extra audible frame. Initial and repeat ranges may overlap or be disjoint. A two-frame repeat does not imply silence.
